Who We Are

Velavaa Tours is a premier travel agency dedicated exclusively to showcasing the majestic beauty, profound spirituality, and vibrant culture of South India. With years of experience and deep local knowledge, we craft journeys that transcend ordinary tourism.


Whether you're looking to sail the backwaters of Kerala, explore the ancient temples of Tamil Nadu, or relax in the misty hills of Ooty and Kodaikanal, we have the perfect itinerary for you.


We pride ourselves on our partnerships with top-tier providers like Yash Holidays, Manikuttan Jeep Safari, Manju Holidays and more to ensure your comfort and safety every step of the way.
